# Unified reports v3

- Keep one Reports screen with two visible operational reports only:
  - Cashier daily close (thermal 80mm)
  - Manager comprehensive report (A4)
- Hide legacy report tabs from the visible UI while preserving their underlying APIs.
- Keep sales, credit sales, and credit collections conceptually separate.
- Cashier close: sales, invoice count, payment methods, credit sales, credit collections, discounts, tax, invoice-paid amount, expenses, expected cash, signatures.
- Manager report: sales, tax, COGS, gross profit, expenses, net profit, payment methods, collections, receivables, employees, top items, cash flow, audit note.
